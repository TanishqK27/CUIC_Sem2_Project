\section{Key Features}

\subsection{Data Leakage Prevention}

The backtester \textbf{strips \texttt{home\_win} from the row} before passing it to the strategy function. Strategies cannot cheat by looking at the outcome. A dedicated ``spy strategy'' test verifies no strategy ever receives \texttt{home\_win}.

\subsection{Transaction Cost Modeling}

Two cost parameters model real-world betting friction:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item \texttt{cost\_pct} --- Percentage deducted from winning payouts (models bookmaker vig)
    \item \texttt{cost\_flat} --- Flat dollar fee per trade regardless of outcome
\end{itemize}

\textbf{PnL formulas:}
\begin{align*}
\text{WIN:} \quad & \texttt{bet\_size} \times (\texttt{odds} - 1) \times (1 - \texttt{cost\_pct}) - \texttt{cost\_flat} \\
\text{LOSS:} \quad & -\texttt{bet\_size} - \texttt{cost\_flat}
\end{align*}

\subsection{Kelly Criterion Position Sizing}

Instead of flat \$100 bets, Kelly sizes each bet as a fraction of bankroll based on estimated edge:

\[
f^* = \frac{bp - q}{b}
\]

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{@{}clll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Variable} & \textbf{Meaning} & \textbf{Example (odds = 1.95)} \\
\midrule
$f^*$ & Fraction of bankroll to bet & 0.1026 ($\approx$10.3\%) \\
$b$ & Net odds = decimal\_odds $-$ 1 & $1.95 - 1 = 0.95$ \\
$p$ & Estimated win probability & 0.5628 \\
$q$ & Loss probability = $1 - p$ & 0.4372 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{@{}clll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Kelly Fraction} & \textbf{Name} & \textbf{Risk Profile} \\
\midrule
0.25 & Quarter Kelly & Very conservative \\
0.50 & Half Kelly & Moderate (default) \\
0.75 & Three-quarter Kelly & Aggressive \\
1.00 & Full Kelly & Maximum growth (unrealistic) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\subsection{Validation Suite (11 Checks)}

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item \textbf{Schema (5 checks):} Correct columns, valid actions/outcomes, positive bets, valid odds
    \item \textbf{Math (4 checks):} PnL formula correctness, cumulative PnL running sum, bankroll tracking, no overbetting
    \item \textbf{Data Leakage (2 checks):} Outcomes match input data, chronological order
\end{itemize}

\section{PR Review Fixes}

The first PR was reviewed by Tan and returned with feedback across three categories. \textbf{All items have been addressed.}

\subsection{Critical Fixes (C1--C5)}

\textbf{C1. \texttt{except Exception:} silently swallowed ALL errors}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item \textbf{Problem:} \texttt{\_\_init\_\_.py} caught SyntaxError, AttributeError, RecursionError --- not just ImportError.
    \item \textbf{Fix:} Changed to \texttt{except ImportError:} with \texttt{logging.debug()}.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{C2. \texttt{kelly\_bet\_home} had circular edge logic}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item \textbf{Problem:} Confidence = implied\_prob + 5\%. Since Kelly is positive whenever $p > 1/\text{odds}$, and here $p = 1/\text{odds} + 0.05$, the strategy always bets. Tautological.
    \item \textbf{Fix:} Renamed to \texttt{kelly\_bet\_home\_demo} with a prominent WARNING docstring.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{C3. Sharpe/Sortino ratios were mathematically meaningless}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item \textbf{Problem:} (1) Used raw dollar PnL, not \% returns. (2) Annualized with $\sqrt{252}$ (trading days). (3) Sortino used $\text{std}(\text{losses\_only})$.
    \item \textbf{Fix:} (1) Now uses \texttt{pnl / bankroll\_before\_bet}. (2) Annualizes with $\sqrt{365}$. (3) New \texttt{calculate\_sortino\_ratio()} with correct downside deviation:
    \[
    \sigma_d = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} \min(0, r_i)^2}
    \]
\end{itemize}

\textbf{C4. Bankroll could go negative with \texttt{cost\_flat}}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item \textbf{Problem:} \texttt{bet\_size} capped at \texttt{bankroll}, but loss $= -(\text{bet\_size} + \text{cost\_flat})$ could exceed bankroll.
    \item \textbf{Fix:} \texttt{bet\_size = min(bet\_size, max(0, bankroll - cost\_flat))}
\end{itemize}

\textbf{C5. Database fallback silently loaded dummy CSV}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item \textbf{Problem:} When DATABASE\_URL was set but query failed, code \texttt{print()}'d a message and loaded synthetic data.
    \item \textbf{Fix:} \texttt{warnings.warn(..., RuntimeWarning)}. Added \texttt{strict=True} mode that raises.
\end{itemize}
