# Annotated Bibliography: Sports Betting and Prediction Markets

## Academic papers

1. Snowberg, E., & Wolfers, J. (2010). *Explaining the Favorite-Longshot Bias: Is it Risk-Love or Misperceptions?* Journal of Political Economy.  
   Link: https://www.nber.org/papers/w15923  
   Annotation: Canonical FLB decomposition; supports probability-weighting/misperception channels over simple risk-love.

2. Shin, H. S. (1993). *Measuring the Incidence of Insider Trading in a Market for State-Contingent Claims.* The Economic Journal.  
   Link: https://academic.oup.com/ej/article/103/420/1141/5157258  
   Annotation: Foundational structural correction for bookmaker odds under insider risk; useful for no-vig probability extraction.

3. Cain, M., Law, D., & Peel, D. (2000). *The Favourite-Longshot Bias and Market Efficiency in UK Football Betting.* Scottish Journal of Political Economy.  
   Link: https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:bla:scotjp:v:47:y:2000:i:1:p:25-36  
   Annotation: Early evidence of FLB in football fixed-odds markets; still relevant for soccer-bias diagnostics.

4. Berkowitz, J. P., Depken, C. A., & Gandar, J. M. (2017). *A favorite-longshot bias in fixed-odds betting markets: Evidence from college basketball and college football.* Quarterly Review of Economics and Finance.  
   Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1062976916000041  
   Annotation: US NCAA evidence of FLB in moneyline markets; helps define segment-specific priors.

5. Whelan, K. (2026 issue / online 2025). *Market structure and prices in online betting markets: theory and evidence.* Oxford Economic Papers.  
   Link: https://academic.oup.com/oep/article/78/1/90/8244336  
   Annotation: Connects observed pricing bias to competitive structure and demand elasticity, not purely bettor irrationality.

6. Simon, J. (2024). *Inefficient Forecasts at the Sportsbook: An Analysis of Real-Time Betting Line Movement.* Management Science.  
   Link: https://ideas.repec.org/a/inm/ormnsc/v70y2024i12p8583-8611.html  
   Annotation: High-value CLV-adjacent paper; shows line-path inefficiencies despite overall forecast quality.

7. Krieger, K., & Fodor, A. (2013). *Price movements and the prevalence of informed traders: The case of line movement in college basketball.* Journal of Economics and Business.  
   Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0148619513000295  
   Annotation: Demonstrates stronger informational content in closing lines and informed-trader effects by game profile.

8. Tsaris, P. (2021). *Market efficiency and the Greek fixed-odds betting market.* EuroMed Journal of Business.  
   Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/org/science/article/pii/S1450219421000281  
   Annotation: Opening vs closing analysis with persistent market biases; useful caution against CLV-only evaluation.

9. Sauer, R. D. (1998). *The Economics of Wagering Markets.* Journal of Economic Literature.  
   Link: https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:aea:jeclit:v:36:y:1998:i:4:p:2021-2064  
   Annotation: Foundational survey: near-efficiency with recurring conditional anomalies.

10. Levitt, S. D. (2004). *Why are Gambling Markets Organised so Differently from Financial Markets?* Economic Journal.  
    Link: https://academic.oup.com/ej/article/114/495/223/5086012  
    Annotation: Shows books can optimize profit via biased prices against bettor demand rather than perfect balancing.

11. Angelini, G., & De Angelis, L. (2019). *Efficiency of online football betting markets.* International Journal of Forecasting.  
    Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0169207018301134  
    Annotation: Large multi-bookmaker panel; supports consensus and best-odds methodologies.

12. Wolfers, J., & Zitzewitz, E. (2004). *Prediction Markets.* Journal of Economic Perspectives.  
    Link: https://www.nber.org/papers/w10504  
    Annotation: Foundational framework for interpreting market prices as probabilistic forecasts.

13. Berg, J. E., Nelson, F. D., & Rietz, T. A. (2008). *Prediction market accuracy in the long run.* International Journal of Forecasting.  
    Link: https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:intfor:v:24:y:2008:i:2:p:285-300  
    Annotation: Long-run comparison showing persistent forecasting strength of markets vs polls.

14. Angelini, G., De Angelis, L., & Singleton, C. (2022). *Informational efficiency and behaviour within in-play prediction markets.* International Journal of Forecasting.  
    Link: https://www.stir.ac.uk/research/hub/publication/1969462  
    Annotation: In-play event-time evidence of behavioral and informational inefficiency.

15. Diercks, A. M., Katz, J. D., & Wright, J. H. (2026). *Kalshi and the Rise of Macro Markets.* NBER Working Paper 34702.  
    Link: https://www.nber.org/papers/w34702  
    Annotation: Recent evidence that regulated event-contract markets carry real-time forecasting signal.

16. Hubacek, O., Sourek, G., & Zelezny, F. (2019). *Exploiting sports-betting market using machine learning.* International Journal of Forecasting.  
    Link: https://ida.fel.cvut.cz/papers/hubacek2019exploiting.html  
    Annotation: Demonstrates objective-function redesign (de-correlation from market odds + portfolio staking) for profitability.

17. Walsh, C., & Joshi, A. (2024). *Machine learning for sports betting: Should model selection be based on accuracy or calibration?* Machine Learning with Applications.  
    Link: https://doaj.org/article/2e36e6f3ec6a46b7b54308dee2d11c23  
    Annotation: Strong practical result: calibration-first model selection beats accuracy-first selection in betting contexts.

## Industry reports

18. American Gaming Association. (2025). *State of the States 2025.*  
    Link: https://www.americangaming.org/resources/state-of-the-states-2025/  
    Annotation: Current US market size and state-level composition; useful for opportunity sizing.

19. UK Gambling Commission. (2025). *Industry Statistics: Annual report (Apr 2024-Mar 2025).*  
    Link: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/statistics-and-research/publication/industry-statistics-annual-report-financial-year-april-2024-to-march-2025  
    Annotation: Official UK market and channel metrics; useful for regional comparability.

20. Sportradar Integrity Services. (2025). *Integrity in Action: 2024 Global Analysis & Trends.*  
    Link: https://sportradar.com/content-hub/report/integrity-in-action-2024/  
    Annotation: Integrity-risk map by sport/region; supports market-risk filters and governance controls.

21. International Betting Integrity Association. (2025). *2024 Annual Report.*  
    Link: https://ibia.bet/category/integrity-reports/  
    Annotation: Independent suspicious-betting alert statistics and enforcement outcomes.
