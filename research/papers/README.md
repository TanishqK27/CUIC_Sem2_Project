## Prediction Markets

### The Promise of Prediction Markets

- **Authors:** Wolfers, J., & Zitzewitz, E. (2004)
- **Link:** [NBER Working Paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/w10504)
- **Key Findings:**
  - Prediction markets aggregate information effectively
  - Market prices closely approximate event probabilities
  - Thin markets can still be informative
- **Relevance:** Foundational paper on prediction market efficiency

### Prediction Markets: Theory, Evidence, and Applications

- **Authors:** Arrow, K. J., et al. (2008)
- **Link:** [Science](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1157679)
- **Key Findings:**
  - Markets outperform polls for election forecasting
  - Information aggregation is robust
- **Relevance:** Scientific validation of prediction market accuracy

### Manipulating Prediction Markets

- **Authors:** Hanson, R., Oprea, R., & Porter, D. (2006)
- **Link:** [Journal of Prediction Markets](https://doi.org/10.5750/jpm.v1i1.417)
- **Key Findings:**
  - Attempted manipulation is usually corrected by market
  - Manipulators often lose money
  - Market resilience depends on liquidity
- **Relevance:** Understanding market manipulation and resilience

## Sports Betting

### Can Investors Profit from the Prophets?

- **Authors:** Brown, W. O., & Sauer, R. D. (1993)
- **Link:** [Journal of Political Economy](https://www.jstor.org/stable/2138678)
- **Key Findings:**
  - NFL betting markets are largely efficient
  - Public biases exist but are small
- **Relevance:** Baseline for sports betting market efficiency

### Market Efficiency and Behavioral Biases in the Sports Betting Market

- **Authors:** Levitt, S. D. (2004)
- **Link:** [The Economic Journal](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.0013-0133.2004.00173.x)
- **Key Findings:**
  - Bookmakers exploit bettor biases
  - Optimal pricing differs from market clearing
  - Favorites are overbet
- **Relevance:** Understanding bookmaker behavior and public biases

### The Favorite-Longshot Bias in Sports Betting Markets

- **Authors:** Snowberg, E., & Wolfers, J. (2010)
- **Link:** [Journal of Political Economy](https://doi.org/10.1086/648594)
- **Key Findings:**
  - Longshots are systematically overbet
  - Bias persists across sports and time
  - Risk preferences partially explain bias
- **Relevance:** Exploitable bias in betting markets

## Kelly Criterion & Bankroll Management

### A New Interpretation of Information Rate

- **Authors:** Kelly, J. L. (1956)
- **Link:** [Bell System Technical Journal](https://doi.org/10.1002/j.1538-7305.1956.tb03809.x)
- **Key Findings:**
  - Optimal growth strategy for repeated bets
  - Maximizes log wealth
  - Avoids ruin
- **Relevance:** Foundation of our position sizing approach

### Fortune's Formula

- **Authors:** Poundstone, W. (2005)
- **Link:** [Book](https://www.amazon.com/Fortunes-Formula-Scientific-Betting-Casinos/dp/0809045990)
- **Key Findings:**
  - History of Kelly criterion application
  - Practical considerations for implementation
  - Half Kelly often preferred in practice
- **Relevance:** Practical guide to Kelly implementation

### The Kelly Criterion in Blackjack, Sports Betting, and the Stock Market

- **Authors:** Thorp, E. O. (2006)
- **Link:** [Handbook of Asset and Liability Management](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1872-0978(06)01009-X)
- **Key Findings:**
  - Kelly optimal for growth rate
  - Fractional Kelly reduces variance
  - Multi-asset Kelly requires correlation estimation
- **Relevance:** Practical Kelly implementation guidance

## Machine Learning for Prediction

### Advances in Financial Machine Learning

- **Authors:** López de Prado, M. (2018)
- **Link:** [Book](https://www.amazon.com/Advances-Financial-Machine-Learning-Marcos/dp/1119482089)
- **Key Findings:**
  - Proper backtesting methodology
  - Feature importance in finance
  - Avoiding overfitting
- **Relevance:** Applying ML to prediction markets

### The Probability of Backtest Overfitting

- **Authors:** Bailey, D. H., et al. (2014)
- **Link:** [Journal of Computational Finance](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2326253)
- **Key Findings:**
  - Most backtested strategies are overfit
  - Multiple testing increases false discovery rate
  - Proper methodology is critical
- **Relevance:** Avoiding overfitting in strategy development

## Arbitrage

### Limits of Arbitrage

- **Authors:** Shleifer, A., & Vishny, R. W. (1997)
- **Link:** [Journal of Finance](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1997.tb03807.x)
- **Key Findings:**
  - Arbitrage is costly and risky
  - Capital constraints limit arbitrageurs
  - Mispricings can persist
- **Relevance:** Understanding why arbitrage opportunities exist
